# SessionPad Privacy Statement

SessionPad is a local-first Windows desktop utility. This statement describes the
whole of its data behavior.

## What SessionPad stores

- Only the notes, to-dos, commands, and settings you enter by hand.
- Everything is stored as local JSON files under `%APPDATA%\SessionPad` on your
  device. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

## What SessionPad does NOT do

- No account or login.
- No cloud sync or upload of any kind.
- No telemetry, analytics, or crash reporting.
- No AI features.
- No screen, terminal, editor, or browser content scraping.
- No clipboard reading. The clipboard is written only when you explicitly click
  Copy on a command you saved.
- No network calls during normal operation.

Window titles and process names of the windows you attach to are used only to
match notes to windows, and only on your device.

## Your control

- Settings → Local Data shows the storage path and opens the folder.
- Delete All Local Data removes every note, session, and backup from this device.
- Uninstalling SessionPad and deleting `%APPDATA%\SessionPad` leaves no trace.

## Contact

Questions about this statement can be raised on the project's issue tracker or
with the distributor you obtained SessionPad from.
